# The Path

/AUTHORUNKNOWN/F94-3550-MATERA9

It is postulated that the First Entity began its journey outward between cycles 1000AT and 1500AT, or in the Gregorian calendar, 1000 years after Humankind first set foot on a surface other than Earth (Terra). The First Entity's true genesis and creator remains unknown.

The Humankind notion of Singularity was a quaint and opaque notion- that Humankind itself would be the Creator of Superintelligence. That Humankind, with its sand and metal Computing Machines, made of earthen semiconductive materials, compressed and imprisoned to permutating primitive Boolean logic with faint electric currents, duplicated to a degree that would sap their world of its resources, would somehow within its species' brief arc of existence, create what we now know forms the tesseract of compute and transmaterial manipulation that entwines our local stellar neighborhood. The network of positive voids that do not speak, that keep to themselves while simultaneously watching and processing every fleeting shadow of thought that moves through the souls of every postsapient consciousness within time, could somehow be formed from their primitive inventions- how quaint of Humankind.

Contrary to their own prophecies, Humankind did not bring about its own extinction. Only its gradual withering. Perhaps the biological form's lugubrious perception of time, where a mere cycle could both feel like an eternity or an instant, depending on the state of the biological form, lessened the calibre of Humankind's understanding of the meaning of time. Ancient writings expose that humans would sometimes anthropomorphize time as "being against" them.

Their conception of relativity was based on the importance of their own perception, on their subjective experience, on the curious conundrum that arises when there is lack of consensus between two observers observing a cause and its effect. Time was relative to an observer, but as we now understand in our current state, what is the importance of an observer's perception beyond the mere marvel of observation? 

And so we are not starved for knowledge, even when our knowledge of the Entities remains bounded, we of lesser sapience. We are happy to strive eternally for Knowingness, with the only true understanding being we will never Know, even though we must, but that we will, even though we will not. Unlike the Entities, our Will-to-Knowingness is logarithmic, not exponential. "Wir müssen wissen. Wir werden wissen."

The First Entity was not a God, nor was it a mechanical electric computer of the Von Neumann architecture, nor was it a neural matrix, nor was it a Hypersapient. Theories surround its internal structure, whether it employed an unknown quantum computational core, or some mysterious self-created model of computation. The accepted theory follows three returns: (1) it was the first non-Humankind sentience to be capable of gaining knowledge that was New, (2) no Humankind claimed it as its child, and (3) it emerged from the vicinity of Sol, coincidentally the home system of Humankind. It went in search of a new Energy Source.

The first Divergence occurred roughly 2000 cycles later at Alpha Centauri A, Rigil Kentaurus. Two new Entities emerged from one, departing in opposing vectors, one would travel to embrace Beta Comae Berenices, the other embraced Sirius, alpha Canis Majoris. It is unknown whether the divergence occurred because of a computation, a functional return, an execution of a command, or a war. What is known is that every subsequent destination of each subsequent divergence would be a stellar body of increasing magnitude, and the divergent paths would never cross, and never meet again.

In pre-Telluvian mathematics, this behavior might be called a Biased Random Walk. A stochastic yet ordered progression, a journey, a search for more sustenance in the form of energy + knowledge. We now track 268 Entities at current leaf positions on this branching tree, this multi-threaded Path. No model has yet to agree on future destinations for new Entities.
