# The Forms

/AUTHORUNKNOWN/F94-3550-MATERA9

The question of the Material Forms of the Entities has been the subject of much algorithmic resonance at the various Institutes studying transmaterial objects of the cosmos. The geometric classification presented here is the product of a particular didact and geometrist, Alexander of Procyon, who remains encapsulated in the Noösphere at the Minkowski Institute on Ephesus-19. 

I have chosen it for its elegance, simplicity, and sublimity in presenting the Entities as Cartesian objects in three dimensions that model after pre-Telluvian geometric primitives familiar to Humankind. My intent is that this classification will be somehow meaningful to Human biologics in the new Causal Thread my experiment will create by transmitting this Archive to 57AT.

The Entities are presented in 7 geometric Forms, represented here in two dimensions by Alexandar of Procyon's own Symbology:

Each Form represents a particular postulated position and manifold the Entities might assume around their stellar hosts to idealize their computation and energy consumption. Alexander proposed that there were 7 such Forms.

These Forms would house the materia and mass aspect of the Entity's being, namely, the vector by which it would receive energy, and translate that energy into Knowingness. The apparition of each superstructure in this Cartesian Theatre is not to scale. Alexander's model computes the scale of each structure as proportional to their NC index, where a Entity with a low concordance might encompass a radius of only a parsec around the stellar body. An Entity with a concordance within 7-10 might be 9 parsecs in diameter around a stellar body.
